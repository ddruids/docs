title: "Colors"
---

Bungee's color system is designed to be **bold, energetic, and instantly recognizable**. Each color plays a specific role in communicating movement, clarity, and confidence across our touchpoints.\
The system is structured in two layers:

- **Core Colors**: Raw palette primitives. Never used directly in UI.
- **Semantic Colors**: Role-based tokens that reference Core Colors and flip per theme mode. These are the components that are consumed.

## Core Colors (Primitive)

Do **not** use raw palette values directly in UI; they exist only to build semantic tokens.
