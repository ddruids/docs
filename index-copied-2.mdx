## Brand strategy

Bungee is the simplest way to move value across chains. We strip away the complexity of bridging and routing so users experience crypto the way it should be: direct, fluid, and intuitive. Bungee exists to make transfer, conversion, and interaction across ecosystems feel effortless.

\
Our visual identity reflects this: bold yet friendly, precise yet playful, always suggesting motion. We make the complex world of interoperability feel light, seamless, and fun.

## Personality

Bungee's brand personality is **open-minded,**  always welcoming experimentation and adapting to the evolving nature of on-chain ecosystems. It is **fun and expressive** with motion and bold colors.\
The brand voice is **bold and clear**, structured to communicate and deliver value,  especially where financial and technical clarity matters

## Brand values

The four values below shape every product decision. When in doubt, return to them.

- **Frictionless movement:** Everything we design should feel like it flows or transitions naturally. If a flow has a stutter, we redesign the flow; we don't decorate around the friction.
- **Precision and clarity:** The experience should deliver value as easily as possible. Numbers are exact. Labels mean what they say. Confirmations confirm.
- **Playfulness with purpose:** The visual system invites creativity while remaining thoughtful and intentional. Motion, color, and shape carry brand expression, but never at the expense of comprehension.
- **Confidence in complexity:** We lean into scale, liquidity, and routing logic, but simplify the surface. The product can be sophisticated underneath; the surface should feel calm.

## Logo

The logo captures transfer, conversion, and motion through geometric form. It is rooted in circular geometry, allowing it to pair seamlessly with rounded shapes, arcs, and curves throughout the system.

- **Typography is the primary logo.**
- Supporting shapes and compositions emphasize motion, but never overpower it.

More than just a symbol, movement is our foundation; every element should feel like it's going somewhere.
